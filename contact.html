Notice periods

Custom cakes, cheesecakes and ice-cream are usually best with about 48 hours. Cookies, brownies, cinnamon rolls, Korean buns and tiramisu are happy with about 24 hours. Bigger party orders are best a week ahead.

Before you message

Contact questions

Which channel is fastest?

WhatsApp, because it keeps the whole conversation in one thread — photos, dates and confirmations included. Instagram DMs and email are checked too, just slightly less often.

Can I call instead?

You can, but if the mixer is running you may get a call back instead. A message with your order details is usually the quickest way to get a yes.

How will I know my order is confirmed?

You’ll get a message with the price, the pickup or delivery slot and the payment details. Your order is booked in once you confirm that message.

Can I change my order after sending it?

Yes, if it isn’t baked yet — just send a new message. Once a custom cake is decorated or in the oven, changes may not be possible. See our Terms & Conditions.

How is my information handled?

Only what’s needed to bake and deliver your order, and nothing is sold. The full details are in our Privacy Policy.
