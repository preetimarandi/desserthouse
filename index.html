Questions

Before you order

How do I place an order?

Two ways. Either build a bag on this site and tap Send on WhatsApp, or message us directly on WhatsApp at our number / Instagram @desserthouse_us. Tell us the treat, quantity and the date you need it.

How much notice do you need?

About 48 hours for custom cakes, cheesecakes and ice-cream, and 24 hours for cookies, brownies, rolls and buns. Bigger party orders are best placed a week ahead.

Is there a minimum order?

No strict minimum — order one box of cookies or one dozen. For deliveries, a small-order delivery charge may apply; pickup is always free.

Can you make eggless or less-sweet versions?

Yes. Eggless, reduced-sugar and nut-free versions are available for most items. Just mention it in your message so the recipe is adjusted before baking.

How do I pay?

You’ll get the total on chat before baking starts. Payment details are shared there, and custom orders are confirmed once the advance is received.

Do you deliver?

Pickup from our cloud kitchen is always free. Delivery is available around the neighbourhood for a small charge, and we’ll confirm whether your area is covered when you order.

Allergies

Everything is baked in one kitchen that handles wheat, dairy, eggs, nuts and soy. Do tell us about allergies so we can advise honestly — but please note we can’t guarantee a completely allergen-free bake.

Got a sweet idea in mind?

Tell us the occasion, the flavour and the date — we’ll take it from there. Orders are taken over WhatsApp and Instagram, and the kitchen answers as soon as the oven timer allows.
